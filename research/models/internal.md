# Internal Geography Model

## Summary
Builds a relational map from the text alone (who is north of whom, travel days, relative positions of Zarahemla, Nephi, Bountiful, Desolation, etc.) without committing to modern GPS coordinates. Often used as a neutral base before testing external models.

## Method
1. Extract directional and distance constraints.
2. Build a graph of places and paths.
3. Test external models for consistency against the graph.
