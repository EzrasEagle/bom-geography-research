# Baja California Model

## Summary
Places Book of Mormon geography primarily on the Baja California peninsula (and nearby), emphasizing climatic match for Jerusalem-origin seeds, peninsular “narrow” geography, and seas on both sides. Landing often associated with Mediterranean-like NW Baja or related coasts.

## Core map (typical)
| Feature | Common proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Land southward | Southern Baja |
| Land northward | Northern Baja / toward Alta California |
| Narrow neck | Narrow peninsular sections |
| River Sidon | Seasonal drainages / specific arroyos (debated) |
| Landing | NW Baja / Pacific or Gulf-adjacent Mediterranean zone |
| Cumorah | Northern peninsula highlands (model-specific) |

## Key claims
- Seeds from Jerusalem “grow exceedingly” implies similar climate/latitude → Mediterranean SW North America.
- Peninsula naturally provides seas east and west.
- Limited geography without requiring Mesoamerican urban horizons.

## Strengths claimed
- Climate/seed argument.
- Dual seas and peninsular shape.
- Alignment with some travel-time and wilderness motifs.

## Criticisms commonly raised
- Population density and large-city descriptions.
- Jaredite time depth and archaeology on Baja.
- Sidon as a major river vs. Baja hydrology.

## Primary sources (seed)
- Rosenvall materials / “A New Approach to Book of Mormon Geography” style arguments.
- Seed/climate essays (e.g. plant physiology arguments popularized in Baja discussions).
