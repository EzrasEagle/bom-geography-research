# South America Models

## Summary
Family of models placing landing in Chile (or nearby) and extending through Andean or broader South American regions. Some are hemispheric hybrids; others attempt limited Andean geographies.

## Core map (highly variable)
| Feature | Common proposal (classic) |
| --- | --- |
| Landing | Chilean coast (~33°S climate analogy) |
| Land southward / northward | Andean corridor variants |
| Narrow neck | Panama isthmus in hemispheric versions |

## Key claims
- Early statements (e.g. Frederick G. Williams note) about landing at 30° south.
- Southern hemisphere Mediterranean climate band near Chile as seed-climate match.

## Notes for catalogers
Treat as multiple sub-models; do not collapse all South America proposals into one map when recording verse claims.
