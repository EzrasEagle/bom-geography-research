# Levantine crops — Baja, SoCal, Gulf sides

## Date palms (*Phoenix dactylifera*, incl. Medjool / Israeli methods)
**Strong modern production:** Coachella Valley (CA); Mexicali Valley (Baja California); San Luis Río Colorado (Sonora); historical Jesuit mission oases across Baja.  
**Climate need:** Hot arid summers, irrigation, mild winters.  
**Weaker match:** Humid Gulf of Mexico coastal plain for premium dry-climate date quality.

## Mediterranean suite (olives, grapes, figs, pomegranates, Near Eastern grains)
**Strong match:** Coastal/near-coastal southern California; NW Baja (e.g. Valle de Guadalupe wine region); microclimates in Baja oases.  
**Chile band (~32°S):** Also a Mediterranean analog used by South America landing arguments.

Link to 1 Ne 18:24 model claims. Distinguish *modern irrigation agriculture* from ancient dryland/oasis practice in notes.
