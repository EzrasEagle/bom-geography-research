These live in **localStorage** in the preview browser and may differ per session:

- `bom-atlas-route-associations-v1` — user edits to routes
- `bom-atlas-model-map-packs-v1` — dragged layouts per model
- `bom-atlas-reader-tags-v3` — personal verse tags
- `bom-atlas-user-associations-v1` — one-click Reader associations

**To keep personal edits:** use Map Lab **Export pack** (JSON) and commit under `data/model-packages/`, or paste route JSON into `src/data/route-associations.ts`.
